HOW THE WEEK RUNS
MON · EMPATHIZE
Notice and listen. One talks, one listens, then swap.
TUE · DEFINE
Sort what you heard. Pick the one problem to fix.
WED · IDEATE
Four ideas in four minutes. Yes, and — never no, but.
THU · PROTOTYPE
Build something rough you can hold up and point at.
FRI · TEST
Pitch it to a real person. Write down what they say.
Design thinking sequence adapted from the Stanford d.school five-mode model. Weekly extensions are d.school-style redesign challenges. Skills Focus competencies mapped to the CASEL framework — social awareness, responsible decision-making, self-awareness, self-management and relationship skills, all five across every week. © 2026 Upshift Learning.
