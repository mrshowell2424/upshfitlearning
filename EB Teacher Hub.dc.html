🆕 New from the EB Writing Workshop
Classroom activities, Nov 2026
Key ideas: short intro paragraph (tag, summary, claim), body paragraphs (reason, intro to evidence, evidence, justification), and spiral review so skills stick. Grammar goes direct instruction, then a quick game, then real writing.
GRADES 2–4 Sentence Strip Sort Partners sort sentences from a short story into who wanted something, what got in the way, and what happened next.
GRADES 2–4 Tag First Students write the title, author, and genre on a sticky note before writing. A fast start for reluctant writers.
GRADES 3–4 Picture Mystery Name a suspect, back it with one text clue and one picture clue, then write a different theory.
GRADES 3–4 Island Debate Groups decide who gets a limited number of spots, then write one sentence explaining why.
GRADES 2–4 Grammar Board Game Shoots-and-ladders board. Students roll, decide if the verb tense is right, and explain the fix.
GRADES 2–4 Tense Switch Rewrite a sentence’s verb to match a past, present, or future card. Quick warm-up.
GRADES 2–4 Discussion Starters Sentence frames like “I agree because...” help students talk about text before writing.
