How the Maze works

1. Read silently
Start at the top. Read the whole story in your head — no reading out loud.
2. Circle one word
Every time you see three words in a box, circle the one that makes sense.
3. Keep going
Do not stop or go back. Work until time is called — 3 minutes per passage.

Practice together

Max has a red kite. He likes to fly it in the park / paper / pink when the wind is strong. The kite goes up / us / an high over the trees.
Talk it through: only one word keeps the sentence making sense. The other two are traps.
TEACHER SCRIPT
“When I say begin, read the story silently. When you come to a box, circle the word that makes the most sense. Keep reading until I say stop. Ready — begin.”

Scoring the Maze

Adjusted score = C − (I ÷ 2)
C = correct circles. I = incorrect circles.
Count only items the student reached. Items left blank after the last one attempted are not scored; blanks skipped in the middle count as incorrect.
WHAT TO WATCH FOR
Fast but wrong → comprehension, not decoding.
Slow but accurate → build fluency next.
Stops mid-page → endurance and stamina.
