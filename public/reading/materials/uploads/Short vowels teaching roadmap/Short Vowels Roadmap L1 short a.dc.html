One vowel closed in by a consonant keeps its short sound. In a closed syllable, a says /a/.

Oral Reading

4 min

Sam and the Tan Cat

Sam had a tan cat. The cat sat on a mat by the back step. Sam had a bag of ham in his lap. The cat ran fast to Sam and sat up. Sam gave the cat a bit of ham. Then the cat had a nap on the mat. Dad had a map and a hat. Dad and Sam ran to the van. The tan cat ran to the van, too!
