9 · READ IT OUT LOUD · TURNING THINGS AROUND
To convert a room into a shop, you turn it into something new. Put a truck in reverse and it turns back the way it came. If you invert a cup, you turn it upside down. A sign can divert traffic and turn cars down a side street. Two friends in a conversation turn words back and forth. Every one of these words has a turn inside it.
