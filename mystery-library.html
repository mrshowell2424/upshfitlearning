36 cases. One per school week. Teacher answers are marked in green.

Week 1
📚 library

The Case of the Library Mess

On Monday morning, Ms. Park opened the school library and gasped. Books were all over the floor! She looked for clues. A trail of sawdust led from the shelf to the class pet cage. The cage door was open, and Nibbles the hamster was gone. Coach Dan had been in the gym all morning with the 5th grade. Leo, the student helper, had stayed home sick. Behind the beanbag, Ms. Park found Nibbles snuggled in a nest of torn paper. "Nibbles!" she said. "You wanted a story, didn’t you?"

Who made the mess in the library?
  • 🐹 Nibbles the hamster: sawdust trail, open cage, nest of paper
  • 🏀 Coach Dan: in the gym all morning
  • 🙋 Leo the helper: home sick
Answer: Nibbles the hamster made the mess.
Key proof:
  • "A trail of sawdust led from the shelf to the class pet cage."
  • "The cage door was open, and Nibbles the hamster was gone."
  • "…Nibbles snuggled in a nest of torn paper."
Clears:
  • Coach Dan: "…had been in the gym all morning with the 5th grade."
  • Leo: "…had stayed home sick."
This shows Nibbles was out of his cage because the door was open and his sawdust was on the floor. Because of this, Nibbles is the one who made the mess.
