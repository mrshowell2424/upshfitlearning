9 · READ IT OUT LOUD · CARRYING THE NEWS
When you transfer a box, you carry it from one place to another. When you refer to a book, you carry an idea from its page into your talk. Teachers hold a conference to bring ideas together. If you prefer one plan, you carry it ahead of the rest. Two plans that differ do not carry the same weight. A good writer will offer a reference for each fact.
