American Revolution choice board

How did this period reshape the relationship between people and government, and what does it reveal about freedom? Students choose one activity.

Instructions for students

  1. Choose one activity from the board.
  2. Show a deep understanding of the American Revolution and its impact on the relationship between people and government.
  3. Use the materials and resources provided to support your research and creation.
  4. Be ready to present your work and explain your process and findings to the class.

Standards

  • 8.H.7 Understanding the outcome of the American Revolution and its impact on national independence and relationships.
  • 8.H.6 Recognizing key events and figures that influenced the Revolution's course and outcome.
