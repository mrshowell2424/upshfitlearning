ANSWER KEY · LEVEL 3 ·
The final y spelling rule
THE RULE
Change the y to i before most endings. Keep the y before -ing and when a vowel comes before it.
SPELLING · ACCEPT ANY FOUR
happier · happiest · easier · busiest · prettier · luckiest · playing · staying · praying · enjoying · delaying · obeying · happy · easy · busy · pretty · lucky · play
DICTATION · READ TWICE, THEN COPS
It was the happiest day of the week.
