ANSWER KEY · LEVEL 4 · REALLY BIG WORDS
in → im, il, ir
THE RULE
The prefix in- means not. It changes to im-, il- or ir- to match the letter that follows.
SPELLING · ACCEPT ANY FOUR
inactive · incorrect · invisible · indirect · informal · incomplete · impossible · impatient · imperfect · immature · immobile · impolite · illegal · illegible · illogical · irregular · irrational · irresponsible
